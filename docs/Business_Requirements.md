# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## Business Questions

### 1. Sales Analytics

- What are the total sales, revenue, and profit?
- How have sales changed over time (daily, monthly, yearly)?
- Which region generates the highest sales?
- Which store performs the best?
- Which product category generates the highest revenue?
- Which products generate the highest profit?
- Which products have declining sales?
- What are the peak sales periods?
- Which promotions increase sales the most?



## 2. Customer Analytics

- Who are the top customers based on revenue?
- Which customers purchase most frequently?
- Which customers have the highest lifetime value?
- Which customers should receive loyalty discounts?
- Which customers are at risk of churning?
- What are the purchasing patterns of different customer segments?
- Which age groups contribute the most sales?



## 3. Product Analytics

- Which products are best-selling?
- Which products are least-selling?
- Which products generate the highest profit?
- Which products have the highest customer ratings?
- Which products receive the most reviews?
- Which products are frequently purchased together?
- Which products have the highest return rates?



## 4. Inventory Analytics

- Which products are running low on stock?
- Which products are overstocked?
- Which products need immediate restocking?
- What is the inventory turnover rate?
- Which warehouses have the highest inventory value?



## 5. Supplier Analytics

- Which suppliers deliver products on time?
- Which suppliers provide the best-quality products?
- Which suppliers have the highest defect rates?
- Which suppliers are associated with the highest return rates?



## 6. Transportation & Logistics Analytics

- Which courier has the fastest delivery time?
- Which courier has the highest delay rate?
- Which regions experience the most delivery delays?
- What is the average delivery time?
- What is the total transportation cost?
- Which shipping method is most efficient?


## 7. Returns & Refund Analytics

- Which products are returned most frequently?
- What are the main reasons for product returns?
- Which product categories have the highest return rate?
- Which suppliers' products are returned the most?
- Which courier is associated with the most damaged deliveries?
- How much revenue is lost due to returns?
- What is the average refund amount?



## 8. Payment Analytics

- Which payment method is used most frequently?
- What is the payment success rate?
- Which payment methods have the highest failure rate?
- What is the total refund amount?



## 9. Business Recommendations

The system should automatically recommend:

- Customers eligible for loyalty discounts.
- Products that should be restocked.
- Products that should be discontinued.
- Suppliers that need performance review.
- Couriers that require improvement.
- Inventory optimization suggestions.
- Promotional opportunities for slow-moving products.
