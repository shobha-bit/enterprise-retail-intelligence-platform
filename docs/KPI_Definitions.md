# KPI Definitions

## Overview

This document defines the key performance indicators used in the Enterprise Retail Intelligence Platform.

The KPIs are designed to provide a consistent view of sales, orders, returns and inventory performance across the Power BI dashboards.

## Sales KPIs

### Total Sales

**Definition:** Total revenue generated from recorded sales transactions.

**Business Purpose:** Used to measure overall sales performance.

### Average Sales

**Definition:** Average sales value across recorded order transactions.

**Business Purpose:** Helps understand the typical sales value of transactions.

### Total Orders

**Definition:** Total number of recorded orders or order transactions used in the dashboard analysis.

**Business Purpose:** Measures overall order activity.

## Return KPIs

### Total Returns

**Definition:** Total number of returned products or return transactions recorded in the returns data.

**Business Purpose:** Measures the overall level of product returns.

### Return Rate

**Definition:** Percentage of orders associated with returns.

**Business Purpose:** Helps evaluate return behavior and identify potential product or operational issues.

**Calculation:**

`Return Rate = Total Returns / Total Orders × 100`

## Inventory KPIs

### Total Inventory Stock

**Definition:** Total quantity of inventory stock recorded across products and warehouses.

**Business Purpose:** Provides an overview of available inventory.

### Reorder Level

**Definition:** The stock level at which inventory may require replenishment.

**Business Purpose:** Helps identify products that may require restocking.

### Inventory Status

**Definition:** Classification of inventory based on available stock compared with the required reorder level.

**Business Purpose:** Helps identify inventory that requires attention.

## Customer KPIs

### Customer Sales Contribution

**Definition:** Sales generated by an individual customer or customer segment.

**Business Purpose:** Helps identify high-value customers.

### Order Frequency

**Definition:** Frequency at which customers place orders during the analyzed period.

**Business Purpose:** Helps understand customer purchasing behavior.

## Product KPIs

### Product Sales

**Definition:** Total sales generated by an individual product.

**Business Purpose:** Helps identify high-performing and underperforming products.

### Times Sold

**Definition:** Number of recorded order transactions associated with a product.

**Business Purpose:** Helps measure product purchasing frequency.

## KPI Consistency

KPI definitions are kept consistent across SQL analysis and Power BI reporting to ensure that dashboard values can be validated against the underlying analytical data.

The KPI values shown in the README and dashboards represent the current project dataset and dashboard state.
